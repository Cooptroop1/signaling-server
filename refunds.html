Refunds

Names are digital and land on your account as soon as they are paid for. Once the name is yours, there is no cooling-off refund just because you changed your mind. Moose packs are the same once the coins are on your account.

We will refund

  • You paid and the name did not apply to your account.
  • You were charged twice for the same name or the same moose pack.
  • You paid for a name that was already sold.
  • You bought moose and they never appeared on your account.

We will not refund

  • You bought a name, got it, then listed or sold it.
  • You spent moose on a name that applied.
  • You typed the wrong name and it was free to claim.
  • Stripe or bank fees on a used-name sale after it completed.

Card listings under £10,000 go through Stripe. Bigger listings are arranged with admin.

Message admin from Moose shop with the name and the email you paid with.
