Privacy

Anonomoose is a phone-to-phone chat. Live rooms are not stored after they burn. This page is what we actually keep.

Chat rooms

Messages, photos, voice and video in a room travel between phones (or a short encrypted backup if there are more than 4 people). Close the tab, refresh, leave for 20 seconds, or the last person out: the room and the code burn. We do not keep a chat history.

Accounts

If you sign up we store your email login, display name, public keys, owned names, friend links, and sealed notes until they are opened or burned. We do not read sealed notes; they are encrypted on your phone.

Payments

Card payments go through Stripe. We store the name you bought, the amount, and Stripe’s payment id so we can apply the name and handle a refund if the name did not apply. We do not store full card numbers.

Device

A PIN, Moose Book and panic settings stay on this phone only. Push subscriptions sit on the signaling server so we can ping you for a note or call.

Contact

Paid name deals: message admin from Moose shop. For this policy: the site operator of anonomoose.com.
